Upload these two files to the ROOT of the Digibox-AR repository:

1. ar-box.glb
2. poster.png

They must be beside index.html, not inside another folder.

The existing index.html already requests:
  src="ar-box.glb?v=5"
  poster="poster.png?v=5"

After uploading both files, wait 1-2 minutes and refresh the GitHub Pages URL.
If the old page remains cached, use Ctrl+F5 or change v=5 to v=6 in index.html.
